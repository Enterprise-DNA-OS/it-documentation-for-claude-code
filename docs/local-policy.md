# Local documentation policy

Configure the business name, responsible operator and approved private evidence archive before live use.

This base treats access reviews and backup restore tests older than 90 days as due. Ninety days is an example business policy, not a statutory period. Procedure reviewers choose the next review date. The renewals read looks ahead 60 days; attention highlights renewals within 30 days. Critical assets need at least one currently reviewed linked runbook. Set criticality explicitly after importing equipment.

Record an evidence reference rather than copying personal information. Use separate databases for separate organisations. Restrict operating system access, encrypt disks and backups, and test database recovery. Drafts, reports and exports are private records. Review them before any manual sharing.

The base keeps historical record content in its audit history. Decide a lawful retention policy covering the database, history, exports and backups. There is no automatic deletion; a retention warning requires an operator-led review and separately authorised disposal or anonymisation. A future retention review date is not a legal basis for keeping information.
