IT Glue's official Enterprise list rate is US$44 per user per month on a 36-month term, with a five-user minimum, onboarding and possible taxes. Fifty users imply US$26,400 a year before extras. This is a transparent list-price scenario, not a verified NZ/AU invoice. No customer usage or invoice was established in accessible local customer and CRM context. No live business database was queried. Search intent is qualitative, not measured search volume.
